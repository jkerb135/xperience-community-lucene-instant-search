Before JavaScript: the form submits with GET on Enter, the cards and page links are real anchors, the chips name the URL's filters. Everything else is a skeleton the client swaps out on its first response; the client never paints its own skeleton over server content. A widget placed above the results (stats, sort) cannot know the total yet and stays a skeleton; pagination below them gets real links.
